# Metanomic: a guide for AI assistants and prospective clients

> Metanomic designs, integrates, and runs custom AI agents that use an organization's knowledge and tools to complete defined tasks within agreed permissions. People retain judgment and approval over consequential actions.

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Official website: https://metanomic.ai/

## What Metanomic does

AI agents that do the work. Your people make the calls.

We design, integrate, and run custom agents that use your knowledge and your tools to finish defined tasks, inside the permissions you set.

The focus is practical work: finding information, coordinating tasks, handling inquiries, processing documents, and working within business systems. The starting point is a specific workflow and a measurable outcome.

## Agent capabilities

These are service categories, not preconfigured products or guarantees for every implementation. Scope, access, safeguards, and success measures are established for each engagement.

### Knowledge & research agents

For analysts and experts.

Answers your team can trace.

Searches approved sources, compares evidence, and cites every finding.

### Operations agents

For operations.

Routine work that doesn't stall.

Coordinates recurring tasks across systems, chases missing information, and escalates exceptions.

### Customer service agents

For support.

Faster resolutions, cleaner handoffs.

Answers questions, investigates account issues, and passes complex cases to your team with full context.

### Sales & intake agents

For revenue teams.

Every inquiry qualified and logged.

Collects requirements, updates your CRM, and books the next step.

### Document-processing agents

For back office.

Documents in, validated data out.

Extracts and checks information, flags gaps and inconsistencies, and queues follow-up.

### Salesforce agents

For Salesforce organizations.

Agents native to your org.

Investigates cases, recommends resolutions, and updates only the fields you approve.

## How we do it

- **Understand:** We begin with the people, their ambitions, and the work worth amplifying.
- **Design:** We shape the solution around human needs, clear safeguards, and measurable impact.
- **Build:** We put practical AI tools into people's hands, integrated with how they already work.
- **Improve:** We learn from real use, refine the tools, and help their human impact grow.

The delivery path is:

1. **Pilot.** One focused use case, scoped to a measurable outcome.
2. **Production.** Integrated with your systems, knowledge, and permissions.
3. **Operations.** Evaluated, monitored, and improved continuously.

Every agent ships with defined permissions, human approval on consequential actions, and measured results.

## Starting a conversation

Describe one workflow that slows your team down. Useful context includes:

- What starts the work and what a completed result looks like.
- The people, systems, and information involved.
- Where work waits, repeats, or requires judgment.
- What an agent could do and what a person must approve.
- What would make a focused pilot worthwhile.

## Try Theia, our AI workflow advisor

Theia is Metanomic's live AI workflow advisor. Describe a business challenge to explore a possible pilot, including tools, safeguards, and ways to measure it. Visitors can also ask about AI technology and how Theia works.

Try Theia: https://metanomic.ai/demo

Theia can check Calendly appointment availability and offer an in-chat booking form for supported meeting types. A booking requires the visitor's details, security verification, explicit confirmation, and confirmation from Calendly. A displayed time is not a reservation.

During supported in-chat bookings, visitors can generate or write a conversation summary, edit it, and approve attaching it to the appointment. Editing the summary clears its approval. The summary is shared only if approved and the meeting type has a compatible question field.

Theia is a free live demo. Messages are processed by a third-party AI model. Do not share sensitive information. Suggestions from Theia are exploratory, not a binding proposal, delivery commitment, or guarantee.
